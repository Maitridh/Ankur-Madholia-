Project
Overview

"Wrath of Bhasmasure" is a third-person action game set in the mystical land of Maheshpur. Players dodge fireballs and engage in an epic battle against the powerful demon Bhasmasure across four progressively challenging levels.

Showcased at IGDC 2023. One of the earliest indie explorations of Indian mythological IP in the arcade space.

Game Overview

"Wrath of Bhasmasure" is set in the mystical land of Maheshpur. Players experience a gripping narrative where strategy, precision, and reflexes are key.

Objectives
  • Survive each level by dodging fireballs for a set time.
  • Enter portals to progress to subsequent levels.
  • Defeat Bhasmasure by lowering his health to zero in the final level.
